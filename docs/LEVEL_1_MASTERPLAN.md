# Level 1 Master Plan

Updated: 2026-09-12

## Core decision

Level 1 will no longer be treated as a tiny six-episode onboarding section.

The user explicitly wants Haneul to stay inside Level 1 for as long as the content remains genuinely useful. There is no artificial episode cap. Quantity is acceptable when it adds real learning value, story continuity, repetition, or mastery.

Level 2 should not become the escape hatch for unfinished beginner material.

## Level 1 objective

Level 1 should fully cover the practical beginner foundation before the learner advances.

The learner should leave Level 1 able to:
- recognize and use core greetings and introductions
- understand basic sentence order and particles in context
- ask and answer simple personal questions
- talk about school, work, family, food, places, time, routines, likes, dislikes, plans, needs, ability, possession, and simple past/future situations
- handle common public situations such as cafés, stores, transport, classrooms, meeting people, and asking for help
- understand common polite Korean speech patterns
- follow short connected scenes without relying on English every line
- reuse learned expressions across episodes rather than memorizing them once
- make simple story choices in Korean
- build enough listening familiarity that Level 2 can increase speed and reduce support

## Structure

Level 1 should be organized as multiple connected story arcs rather than one story.

Recommended structure:

### Arc 1 — First Encounters
Focus:
- 안녕하세요
- names
- 저는...
- 이에요/예요
- 이/가
- 은/는
- basic yes/no
- 만나서 반가워요
- nationality / school / major / identity

### Arc 2 — Campus Life
Focus:
- classroom objects
- 이것/그것/저것
- 있다/없다
- location
- 에 / 에서
- 뭐 / 어디 / 누구
- asking simple questions
- schedules and classes

### Arc 3 — Café & Food
Focus:
- 주세요
- 하고 / 과 / 와
- counters
- prices
- 좋아하다 / 싫어하다
- 먹다 / 마시다
- ordering
- requests
- 맛있어요
- choices

### Arc 4 — Friends & Family
Focus:
- family vocabulary
- possession
- 의
- age
- people descriptions
- relationship language
- simple honorific awareness
- introducing others

### Arc 5 — Daily Routine
Focus:
- time
- days
- 지금
- 아침 / 점심 / 저녁
- wake / go / study / sleep
- -아요/어요 present tense
- frequency words
- sequencing basic actions

### Arc 6 — Around Town
Focus:
- directions
- transport
- 여기/거기/저기
- 가다 / 오다
- 어디에 있어요
- left/right/front/back
- destination particle 에
- asking for help

### Arc 7 — Shopping & Everyday Needs
Focus:
- 얼마예요
- numbers and counters
- 주세요
- 있다/없다 review
- colors
- sizes
- simple comparisons
- this/that choices
- practical store interactions

### Arc 8 — Likes, Hobbies & Weekend Plans
Focus:
- 좋아해요
- 잘하다 / 못하다
- 하고 싶어요
- hobbies
- weather basics
- invitations
- 같이
- simple future intention
- 갈까요 / 할까요 exposure

### Arc 9 — Yesterday & Tomorrow
Focus:
- simple past
- 았/었어요
- future/intention basics
- -(으)ㄹ 거예요
- yesterday/today/tomorrow
- recounting very short events
- making simple plans

### Arc 10 — Beginner Story Finale
Focus:
- recycle all Level 1 language
- longer scenes
- fewer English prompts
- multi-scene task
- meaningful story decisions
- saved dialogue recall
- mixed listening / reading / reply-choice challenges

## Episode philosophy

Do not cap Level 1 by a fixed episode number.

Use episodes when they justify themselves through:
- new communicative function
- new grammar in context
- new practical vocabulary domain
- meaningful story development
- spaced recycling of old language
- listening adaptation
- learner mistake repair
- cultural/pragmatic distinction

Avoid filler episodes whose only purpose is increasing count.

A realistic first production target can be 40–80 high-value episodes across the arcs, but the system should support far more without redesign.

## Internal progression inside Level 1

Instead of immediately jumping to Level 2, Level 1 itself can have sub-stages:

- L1-A: heavily supported beginner
- L1-B: guided beginner
- L1-C: active beginner
- L1-D: independent beginner
- L1-Finale: consolidation arc

This lets difficulty grow while the learner remains in Level 1.

## Story model

Seoyeon and Minjun remain recurring anchors.

Add recurring side characters gradually:
- classmate
- café worker / friend
- professor or teacher figure
- roommate / sibling / friend
- shop staff / local character

Characters should recur across arcs so the learner remembers people and language together.

Each arc should have its own mini-conflict or objective while remaining part of one connected season.

## Learning loop per episode

Recommended pattern:
1. short cinematic setup
2. listen/read scene
3. tap line for support
4. vocabulary and phrase discovery
5. grammar/use note only when needed
6. learner reply choice
7. micro-consequence
8. scene continues
9. quick recall/reuse
10. episode close with one memorable Korean line saved to journey

Not every episode needs every step rigidly; variety is important.

## Repetition strategy

A new item should not disappear after one appearance.

Important words/structures should recur:
- again after 1–2 episodes
- again in a different context
- again inside a choice
- again in listening without English
- again during review
- again later in the arc

Level 1 completion should mean functional familiarity, not exposure count.

## Unlocking Level 2

Level 2 should unlock only after:
- core Level 1 arcs are completed
- essential beginner functions have been encountered repeatedly
- Personalized Review has enough data
- learner can handle a Level 1 finale with reduced support

Do not require perfect mastery of every item.

## Product rule

The purpose is not to keep learners trapped in Level 1.

The purpose is to make Level 1 complete enough that moving to Level 2 actually feels earned and noticeably different.
