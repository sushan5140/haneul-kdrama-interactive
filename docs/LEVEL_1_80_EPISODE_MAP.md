# Haneul Level 1 — Canonical 80-Episode Map

Updated: **2026-09-12**

## Status

This is the canonical production map for Haneul K-Drama Interactive Level 1.

Fixed size: **80 full story episodes**

Structure:
- **20 current Online King Sejong Institute Beginner 1 curriculum units**
- **4 Haneul episodes per KSI unit**
- **10 connected story arcs**
- **8 episodes per arc**
- recurring leads: **Seoyeon (서연)** and **Minjun (민준)**

This map replaces the old assumption that Level 1 is only six episodes.

Existing Level 1 material should be reused/remapped where it fits instead of deleted automatically.

---

# Curriculum source

Official Online King Sejong Institute Beginner 1 is currently split into:

- Beginner 1A — lessons 1–10
- Beginner 1B — lessons 11–20

Official 2026 curriculum pages used when this map was created:

- 1A: https://www.iksi.or.kr/lms/crse/crseApply.do?crseNo=120757
- 1B: https://www.iksi.or.kr/lms/crse/crseApply.do?crseNo=121171
- Curriculum overview: https://www.iksi.or.kr/lms/main/curriculum.do

KSI defines the learning boundary. Haneul transforms the curriculum into connected drama, contextual listening, reply choices, recycling, and personalized review.

Exact micro-grammar/vocabulary for production scripts should be checked against the current lesson materials during implementation. Do not invent a grammar target only because an episode title sounds compatible.

---

# Four-episode pattern per KSI unit

Every KSI lesson becomes four substantial episodes:

1. **Encounter** — first natural story exposure
2. **Expansion** — same function in a different connected situation
3. **Active Use** — learner must respond/use the Korean
4. **Recycle / Checkpoint** — new material + earlier material returns in-story

The pattern is a production guide, not a rigid UI template. Episodes should still feel like drama.

# ARC 1 — New Faces at Haneul
## KSI lessons 1–2 · Episodes 1–8
Theme: identity, introductions, who someone is / is not

### KSI 1A Lesson 1
**저는 한국 사람이에요** — “I am Korean.”

**Episode 1 — Arrival at Haneul**
- Seoyeon encounters Minjun on the first morning.
- First greetings and basic identity language.
- Heavy English support allowed.

**Episode 2 — The Name Tag Mix-Up**
- A misplaced name tag causes two people to be confused for each other.
- Learner distinguishes names and basic identity statements.

**Episode 3 — Who’s New Here?**
- Seoyeon introduces Minjun to another student.
- Learner selects appropriate self-introduction replies.

**Episode 4 — First Introductions**
- Mini social gathering.
- Recycles greetings, names, nationality/identity language.
- First Level 1 checkpoint.

### KSI 1A Lesson 2
**회사원이 아니에요** — “I am not an office worker.”

**Episode 5 — The Wrong Guess**
- Someone incorrectly assumes Minjun’s role.
- Introduces correcting identity politely.

**Episode 6 — Student, Not Staff**
- Seoyeon helps clear up another misunderstanding on campus.
- Positive vs negative identity statements.

**Episode 7 — Guess Who?**
- Learner identifies people from simple descriptions.
- Reply choices require correcting wrong assumptions.

**Episode 8 — The Welcome Board**
- The group prepares a simple introduction board.
- Recycles Lessons 1–2 together.
- Arc 1 checkpoint.

# Level 1 difficulty curve

## Episodes 1–16 — Supported
- Korean + English visible
- frequent tap support
- short exchanges
- choices heavily scaffolded

## Episodes 17–32 — Guided
- Korean shown first
- translation increasingly optional
- older language appears without warning
- short listening-first moments

## Episodes 33–48 — Active
- learner chooses replies more often
- support appears after attempt
- more cross-episode recycling

## Episodes 49–64 — Reduced Support
- longer exchanges
- fewer automatic glosses
- listening and contextual inference matter more

## Episodes 65–80 — Beginner Independent
- Korean-dominant scenes
- English still available on demand
- choices mix multiple previously learned functions
- finale checks practical comprehension rather than memorized definitions

# Story continuity rules

- Seoyeon and Minjun remain the emotional/story anchors.
- Add side characters gradually; do not introduce a large cast at once.
- Recurring locations should include campus, café/food spot, library, shopping street, station/subway, home/housewarming location, and event venue.
- Every 4th episode is a natural checkpoint, not a detached quiz.
- Every 8th episode closes a mini-arc.
- Earlier decisions may create short callbacks, but branches should rejoin quickly.
- Important Korean should recur across multiple arcs.
- No episode should exist only to increase the count.

# Existing Level 1 migration rule

The current app already contains six Level 1 episodes.

During implementation:
1. inspect those six episodes
2. map reusable scenes/learning targets into Episodes 1–8 where appropriate
3. preserve good dialogue/artwork/logic
4. rewrite only what conflicts with the KSI-aligned map
5. do not delete working content merely because the new plan is larger

---

# Production sequence

Do not attempt to script all 80 at once.

Build in this order:

1. Level 1 data/navigation architecture that supports 80 episodes and 10 arcs
2. migrate/refine existing Episodes 1–6
3. complete Arc 1 (Episodes 1–8)
4. verify story/review/recycling behavior
5. complete Arc 2
6. proceed arc by arc
7. update this map only when a curriculum or story requirement genuinely changes

Google OAuth remains a final-stage task and should not interrupt Level 1 production.
