## Critical correction

Current source Episode 8 is:

**Episode 8 — The Profile Card Check**

It is NOT "The Welcome Board".

Therefore the three previously generated welcome-board images must not be inserted into the current Episode 8. They may be archived as unused concept art, but they are not canonical scene art for the current script.

## Production rule

For every episode from 8 onward:

1. freeze the six-scene script/context
2. write one exact visual brief per scene
3. generate only 3 scene images at a time
4. user reviews the batch
5. revise rejected images before moving forward
6. generate the next 3 scenes
7. integrate only approved images into the app
8. verify the image shown for each scene matches the actual scene text
9. update GitHub after each completed episode or meaningful visual checkpoint

Do not reuse one generic image across unrelated scenes when the scene context materially changes.

# Episode 8 — The Profile Card Check

## Scene 1
Story:
Before orientation starts, Seoyeon and Minjun sit at a campus table with printed profile cards and check them for mistakes.

Visual:
- orientation table on campus
- Seoyeon and Minjun seated together
- several printed profile cards spread on the table
- pens/highlighters
- both visibly checking cards
- no welcome-board easel
- calm pre-orientation atmosphere

## Scene 2
Story:
One profile card incorrectly says Minjun is an office worker.

Visual:
- close/mid shot at the same table
- Seoyeon holding or pointing to Minjun's profile card
- card visually implies the wrong occupation
- Minjun reacts with mild surprise/amusement
- preserve same clothing/location continuity from Scene 1

## Scene 3
Story:
Minjun corrects the card: not an office worker, but a student.

Visual:
- Minjun actively correcting the printed card with a pen
- student ID / campus orientation material can reinforce that he is a student
- Seoyeon watching
- corrected profile card remains the focal object

## Scene 4
Story:
Yuki's profile card has the wrong nationality.

Visual:
- Seoyeon and Minjun reviewing a second profile card
- Yuki's card clearly distinct from Minjun's card
- a small portrait/name card for Yuki
- scene should communicate another information-checking mistake, not a new location
- avoid relying on tiny generated text for meaning; pose/composition should carry the context

## Scene 5
Story:
With the cards corrected, Seoyeon tells Minjun it is his turn to introduce himself.

Visual:
- corrected cards neatly on the table
- Seoyeon gesturing toward Minjun / small orientation group
- Minjun preparing to stand or speak
- transition from desk task to speaking turn
- orientation setting still consistent

## Scene 6
Story:
Minjun gives his complete beginner introduction to the group.

Visual:
- Minjun standing and introducing himself
- Seoyeon nearby, supportive
- small group of students listening
- orientation room/campus setting
- corrected profile cards visible subtly in foreground/background to connect the episode
- clear Arc 1 finale mood without turning into a giant celebration

Batch order:
- Batch 8A: Scenes 1–3
- review/revise
- Batch 8B: Scenes 4–6
- review/revise
- integrate all six approved images

# Character continuity

Seoyeon and Minjun must remain visually consistent within each episode and across adjacent episodes.

Keep:
- approximate age
- face identity
- hairstyle
- general body proportions
- campus/K-drama visual tone

Clothing can change when the story day/location changes, but not randomly between scenes that happen continuously.

---

# Image/context alignment checklist

Before integrating any image:
- Does the location match the scene?
- Are the correct characters present?
- Are they doing what the story says?
- Does the key object/action match the dialogue?
- Does the image accidentally imply a different lesson?
- Does it preserve continuity with the scene before and after?
- Is any generated text essential to comprehension? If yes, redesign the image so the meaning does not depend on text accuracy.

Only approved images are inserted into production.
